## Description

## Stop time. Write the move. Release the consequence.

**ZAWARUDO** is a tactical 2D arena fighter where every player plans inside the
same frozen moment. Record your movement, aim your attack, lock the plan, then
watch the next fraction of a second explode into motion.

Knives, energy shots, fighters, moving platforms and debris keep their position
and velocity across each time stop. A threat can hang in the air for several
turns while both sides decide whether to dodge it, deflect it, or turn it into a
trap.

### Choose your fighter

- **The Duelist** controls space with ricocheting knife volleys.
- **The Rook** commits to high-speed dashes and a brutal front guard.
- **The Pulse** bends trajectories with plasma and electrical feedback.
- **The Eclipse** casts a returning chakram whose third turn changes the fight.

### Play your way

- Tactical 1v1 with simultaneous planning and deterministic execution
- Local matches for 2–4 fighters, including free-for-all and team battle
- Private online 1v1 rooms with hidden plans
- Three CPU skill levels that play by the same information rules as you
- Four arenas with wraparound layouts, hazards, moving geometry and breakable cover
- Keyboard and mouse, gamepad, and landscape touch controls
- Rebindable keys, reduced flashes, high-contrast previews, and separate voice/effects volume
- Free Play sandbox for learning fighters and tuning the feel without a turn clock

This is an active playtest build. Expect balance changes, new polish, and the
occasional sharp edge. Feedback and match reports are welcome.

### Controls

**Keyboard + mouse:** `A` / `D` move · `W` or `Space` jump · mouse aim · hold and
release `LMB` to attack · `Left Shift` lock plan · `R` undo · `T` arm SUPER

Gamepad and touch equivalents are shown in game.
