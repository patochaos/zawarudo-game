## Description

## Plan the move while time is stopped. Then watch it play out.

**ZAWARUDO** is a 2D arena fighter played in turns of frozen time. Both players
plan at once: record a short route, aim a knife throw, lock the plan. Then time
runs for a fraction of a second and both plans play out together.

Nothing resets between turns. Knives, fighters, platforms and debris keep their
position and speed through each stop, so a knife thrown two turns ago can still
be in the air, and both players have to decide what to do about it.

### The Duelist

You play **The Duelist**. Your opponent's route is hidden while you plan, so you
guess where they will be and put knives there. A low charge throws a wide, slow
pair. A full charge throws a fast, tight one.

### What is in the playtest

- **Learn to Play:** three short lessons on the real eight-second timer
- **Start Duel:** a ladder of five CPU opponents, each in its own arena, first to three hits
- **Review and replay:** step through the last turn slowly, or replay the whole match
- **Rematch** the same opponent, or move on to the next one after a win
- **Options:** rebindable keys, high-contrast previews, reduced flashes, separate voice and effects volume
- **Online:** private rooms for two or four players; everyone needs the same build

The question this build is trying to answer: after you plan a move and watch it
collide with the other plan, do you want another match? Use **Send Feedback** in
the menu or on the results screen to prepare an email to **patochaos@gmail.com**.
Say what you tried, what confused you and what you would try next. Match reports
stay on your device unless you attach them.

### Controls

**Keyboard + mouse:** `A` / `D` move · `W` or `Space` jump · mouse aim · hold and
release `LMB` to attack · `Left Shift` lock plan · `R` undo · `T` arm SUPER

Gamepad and touch equivalents are shown in game.
