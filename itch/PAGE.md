## Description

## Stop time. Write the move. Release the consequence.

**ZAWARUDO** is a tactical 2D arena fighter where every player plans inside the
same frozen moment. Record your movement, aim your attack, lock the plan, then
watch the next fraction of a second explode into motion.

Knives, fighters, platforms and debris keep their position
and velocity across each time stop. A threat can hang in the air for several
turns while both sides decide whether to dodge it, deflect it, or turn it into a
trap.

### Master the Duelist

**The Duelist** is your fighter. Predict your opponent’s movement and place
knife volleys across their route. A knife from an earlier turn can still decide
the next one.

### Try the core loop

- **Learn to Play:** three playable lessons with the real eight-second turn timer
- **Start Duel:** straight into Endless Descent with four-way screen wrapping, against a Novice CPU, first to three hits
- **Read the result:** slow last-turn review and full-match replay
- **Play again:** rematch immediately or try the Standard CPU
- **Make it readable:** rebindable keys, high-contrast previews, reduced flashes and separate voice/effects volume
- **Test with a friend:** private online rooms; every player must use the same build

This demo asks one question: did planning a move and watching it collide make
you want another match? Use **Send Feedback** in the menu or results to prepare
an email to **patochaos@gmail.com**. Tell us what you tried, what confused you,
and what you want to try next. Match reports stay local unless you share them.

### Controls

**Keyboard + mouse:** `A` / `D` move · `W` or `Space` jump · mouse aim · hold and
release `LMB` to attack · `Left Shift` lock plan · `R` undo · `T` arm SUPER

Gamepad and touch equivalents are shown in game.
